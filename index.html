RESTVIEW PATIENT CONSOLE

A voice for patients who can only move their eyes

This screen is controlled entirely by looking. Glance toward an option to highlight it, then close your eyes gently for one second to choose it. No hands, taps, or voice needed.

A caregiver should complete the short calibration step once, then hand the console to the patient.

CALIBRATION · STEP 1 OF 5

Look at the gold dot

Hold your gaze on the dot until the ring fills. This teaches the console what “up,” “down,” “left,” and “right” look like for your eyes.
